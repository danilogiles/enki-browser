Protected video

Widevine, for Crunchyroll, Spotify and other streaming sites

Download Widevine from Google?

Widevine is Google's closed-source module that streaming sites use to play protected video. Enki Browser does not include it: this downloads it from Google (update.googleapis.com), checks Google's signature, and keeps it up to date with about one request to Google a day. Google's terms apply. It runs only when a site plays protected video, and you can turn it off here at any time.
