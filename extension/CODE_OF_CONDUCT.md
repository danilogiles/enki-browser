# Code of Conduct

Enki is built by people from different countries, languages and levels of experience. We want
taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone asked a first question once.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain your reasoning when you do.
- Write in the language you are most comfortable in; others can translate.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or content.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions.

## Where this applies

In the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone
represents the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/danilogiles/enkibrowser/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers only) or by direct message to a maintainer. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
